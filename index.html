01 About

I'm a software developer at Struzon Technologies in Coimbatore. I work mostly on engineering automation for structural steel. I designed and built Struzon API Tools, a C# fabrication automation suite of about 31,000 lines. I also built a drawing-to-model QA engine that checks PDF structural drawings against the live Tekla model.

My work covers the full stack, from database schema and backend logic to UI and deployment. Beyond .NET, I build business modules in PHP / CodeIgniter and administer the company's Linux servers and KVM virtualization.

I moved into development from enterprise data operations. That background taught me to care about accuracy and validation, which is why so much of my work is QA tooling.
